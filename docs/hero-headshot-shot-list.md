# Hero headshot — photographer brief

**For:** Barbara Zamost / Zamost Public Relations  
**Use:** Website hero (split layout, ~44% width, full viewport height)  
**Goal:** Senior PR principal — warm and approachable, not corporate-stiff

---

## Deliverables

- **1 hero portrait** — vertical-friendly crop (3:4 or 4:5), shoulders up
- **1 square crop** — LinkedIn / social (optional same session)
- **Files:** Full-res JPG + web-optimized (~1200px wide) JPG or WebP
- **Retouching:** Light only — natural skin, no heavy smoothing

---

## Look & feel

| Do | Avoid |
|----|--------|
| Genuine smile, direct eye contact | Stiff “corporate” pose |
| Soft neutral background (cream, warm gray, blurred outdoor) | Restaurant, booth, busy interiors |
| Professional lighting (soft key + fill) | Harsh overhead or phone flash |
| Solid or subtle texture wardrobe (navy, burgundy, black, cream) | Loud patterns or logos |
| Sharp focus on eyes | Heavy filters or oversaturation |

---

## Wardrobe (bring 2 options)

1. **Primary:** Navy or charcoal blazer over solid top (matches site palette `#243044`)
2. **Alternate:** Burgundy/maroon top with dark layer — continuity with current brand photos

**Jewelry:** Fine to keep hoops/necklace if they read polished, not distracting.

---

## Background options (pick one)

1. **Studio:** Warm off-white or soft gray seamless
2. **Environmental:** Bay Area outdoor — golden hour, soft bokeh (Marin, Embarcadero, garden)
3. **Office-adjacent:** Clean wall, shallow depth of field

Background should blend with site colors: cream `#faf8f5`, navy `#243044`, gold accent `#c4a035`.

---

## Framing notes for web crop

The site crops with `object-fit: cover` in a tall left column. Photographer should:

- Leave **headroom** above hair (≈10–15% of frame)
- Center face slightly **right of frame** (site blends left edge into text panel)
- **No other people** or objects at frame edges
- Capture **chest-up** minimum; full session can include wider shots for About page
