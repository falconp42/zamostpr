Flagship work

Guide Dogs for the Blind

Challenge

Sustain national visibility for a mission-driven organization serving people who are blind or visually impaired — across program launches, advocacy issues, and everyday stories that deserve mainstream attention.

Strategy

Narrative hooks tied to live news cycles — graduation stories, puppy-raising recruitment, rideshare access, and expert commentary editors were already covering.

Approach

SF/Bay Area and national media relations: tailored pitching, spokesperson support, and sustained relationship-building with broadcast, print, and digital outlets.

Result

Coverage across NBC Nightly News, TIME, the Associated Press, KTVU, KPIX, and dozens of regional outlets — plus an ongoing media archive of recent stories.

About

Meet Barbara Zamost

A senior communications strategist with deep roots in the Bay Area, Barbara Zamost brings four decades inside the pitch — from Edelman and Oxford to national nonprofit campaigns. She works as a principal, not a pass-through to junior staff.

Barbara specializes in elevating mission-driven nonprofits and leading consumer products through strategic public relations and communications. She currently serves as SF/Bay Area and national public relations representative for Guide Dogs for the Blind, and provides publicity support for 9/11 Day — the September 11 National Day of Service and Remembrance. Her approach combines rigorous media strategy with authentic storytelling — ensuring that every pitch, press release, and campaign reflects the true heart of an organization.

Based in the vibrant San Francisco Bay Area, she understands the regional media landscape intimately, while maintaining strong national relationships with journalists, editors, and producers across every major platform.

Barbara founded Zamost Public Relations in 1991 after agency stints at Edelman, Duffey Communications, and Sanger Associates. She studied literature and contemporary British theatre at University College, Oxford, and holds a BA in English from Emory University. She is also the author of Handstands in the Sand, a children's book.

Her entertainment work — credited on IMDb as a film publicist — reflects the same skill she brings to nonprofits and consumer brands: building momentum for a timed launch when the deadline is immovable and the story has to land.
